# ACOP Polymer Candidate Audit

Research audit completed 2026-09-03. Phase 2 subsequently inserted only the six approved recommendations while preserving the original three-polymer baseline; no predicted property was imported as measured evidence.

## 1. Executive conclusion

Thirteen exact commercial grades were screened across Nanoscribe, UpNano, and micro resist technology. Six are strong candidates for a later Phase 2 insertion: Nanoscribe IP-n162 and IPX-Clear; UpNano UpOpto, UpSol, and UpBrix 2; and micro resist technology OrmoComp. The existing Nanoscribe IP-S, Nanoscribe IP-Dip2, and UpNano UpPhoto records should remain unchanged until that later implementation pass.

The evidence supports optical or structural two-photon-polymerization use, not adhesive performance. None of the 13 grades qualifies as **Adhesive-capable photopolymer**: no exact-product source located in this audit reports a bond-strength test, bonded-joint geometry, or component-attachment result. Nanoscribe and UpNano describe printing structures directly on substrates, while OrmoComp is used for optical couplers and can be processed with an adhesion promoter; those facts are not equivalent to measured adhesive bonding ([Nanoscribe optics materials](https://www.nanoscribe.com/en/products/optics-manufacturing-2pp-printing-materials/), [UpNano UpSol process](https://www.upnano.com/resins/high-resolution-applications/), [micro resist OrmoComp](https://microresist.de/en/produkt/ormocomp/)).

No exact commercial resin grade attributable to Multiphoton Optics was found. The primary corporate evidence describes Multiphoton Optics as a Heidelberg Instruments subsidiary and a provider of two-photon-polymerization equipment/solutions, not as the manufacturer of a named resin ([Heidelberg Instruments/MPO press release, 2022](https://heidelberg-instruments.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/20220117-EN-PR-MPO-100-Product-Launch_V118.pdf)). It is therefore documented as an investigated ecosystem, not padded into the candidate count.

Optical transmission evidence remains the main insertion risk. Several manufacturers publish spectra but not machine-readable percentage values, or omit specimen thickness. ACOP must retain the original graph/wording and must not calculate apparent attenuation unless percentage, wavelength, and path length are all explicit.

## 3. Source-by-source evidence notes

### URL access verification

Every distinct URL in this report was checked with a normal redirect-following GET on 2026-09-03: **41 distinct URLs = 31 HTTP 200 + 9 HTTP 403 + 1 HTTP 404**. The nine 403 results comprise seven Nanoscribe HTML pages, the ACS PI1M DOI destination and the OpenReview SPG-TED PDF; they are treated as anti-bot/client-restricted access, never as successful public downloads or content verification. The sole 404 is the explicitly labeled unavailable UpNano HubNano page. The 2026 Nanoscribe material guide returned HTTP 200. UpNano PDF redirects were followed to their final manufacturer-hosted documents. HTTP success is used only as reachability evidence; candidate findings come from exact-grade document/page content, not status codes.

Source types and dates are preserved as follows: exact manufacturer product pages are live HTML and undated unless a launch date is cited; the Nanoscribe manufacturer PDF was created 2026-03-03 and modified 2026-03-26 according to its document metadata ([material guide, 2026](https://www.nanoscribe.com/fileadmin/Nanoscribe/PDF/Product-folder/Productfolder-Printing-Materials-2026.pdf)); UpNano sources are manufacturer PDFs, with UpOpto marked revision 08/2026 and the other resolving sheets carrying no reliable publication date used here; OrmoComp and OrmoClear FX are undated exact manufacturer HTML pages, supplemented by the dated 2024 manufacturer flyer and 2025-11-06 platform-validation article; the Multiphoton Optics source is a 2022-01-18 manufacturer press-release PDF.

## 5. Recommended Phase 2 insertion shortlist

1. **Nanoscribe IP-n162** — strongest exact-grade case for high-index integrated photonics and photonic packaging, with explicitly UV-cured n = 1.6221 at 589 nm/20 °C ([product page](https://www.nanoscribe.com/en/products/2pp-printing-materials/photoresin-ip-n162/)).

### External research-resource policy

ACOP now indexes six discovery resources without treating them as materials or property evidence: [IBM Materials Discovery projects](https://research.ibm.com/projects?tag=materials-discovery), [IBM Materials Discovery topic](https://research.ibm.com/topics/materials-discovery), [IBM PatCID](https://research.ibm.com/blog/patcid-tool-for-accelerating-materials-discovery), [MIT polymer-property guide](https://libguides.mit.edu/properties-bymaterial/poly), [NIMS PoLyInfo](https://polymer.nims.go.jp/), and [Chicago 3PDB](https://pppdb.uchicago.edu/). MIT and IBM index pages are discovery-only. PatCID's 81 million structure images and 14 million unique chemical structures are patent-search leads, not experimentally validated polymer products; claims require the originating patent. SPG-TED289M's one million serialized-polymer-graph pretraining samples are not one million commercial, experimentally characterized polymers, and generated candidates require synthesis and qualification.

## 8. Proposed minimal field additions

**Schema conclusion: no new field is justified by the shortlisted evidence.** Every reviewed value can be preserved accurately with ACOP's existing schema ([current ACOP implementation](index.html)):

- Use `exposure_wavelength_nm` only for one explicitly reported exposure wavelength. Preserve a range or non-normalizable exposure description in existing provenance conditions/notes rather than inventing a scalar.
- Use `writing_process` and `process_notes` for printer, objective, build mode, platform restrictions and direct-on-substrate context.
- Use `development_post_cure_process` for developer baths, timings, bake steps and UV/thermal post-cure.
- Use provenance `value_status`, `method`, `conditions`, `notes` and existing calculation metadata to distinguish measured, calculated and predicted values and to preserve liquid, green, UV-cured, thermally cured or unreported specimen state.

No shortlisted source value was found that cannot be represented by those existing fields. Do not add an adhesion-strength field merely because adhesion is a research goal; use existing bond-test fields only after an exact grade has a defined substrate pair, test method, geometry, conditioning and result.

## 11. Phase 3 candidate audit — dual-use TPP and bonding materials (2026-09-04)

### 11.1 Executive finding and method

This phase screened eight exact commercial grades or experimentally specified formulations for the unusually strict intersection of two-photon polymerization (TPP) and genuine bonding. **No candidate is safe to insert now as an `Adhesive-capable photopolymer`.** Four candidates have quantified interface or contact-adhesion tests, but lack the optical, commercial, or component-attachment evidence needed for photonic-assembly selection; the best photonic-wire material has no interface-strength test. Direct writing onto a substrate, surviving development, or functioning as a waveguide was treated as structural microfabrication—not adhesive evidence.

The required discovery resources were checked without importing their contents. [NIMS PoLyInfo](https://polymer.nims.go.jp/) requires registered access for database use and prohibits scraping or mass downloading, so no authenticated search or extraction was attempted. [MIT's polymer guide](https://libguides.mit.edu/properties-bymaterial/poly) and the [IBM projects](https://research.ibm.com/projects?tag=materials-discovery), [topic](https://research.ibm.com/topics/materials-discovery), and [PatCID](https://research.ibm.com/blog/patcid-tool-for-accelerating-materials-discovery) pages were used only as discovery aids. [Chicago 3PDB](https://pppdb.uchicago.edu/) was reviewed as a measured/predictive Tg, Flory–Huggins χ, and cloud-point resource; no calculated output was treated as measurement evidence.

Evidence levels used below are: **E1**, exact-product/formulation primary experiment or exact-grade manufacturer document; **E2**, product-family or system evidence that cannot safely be assigned to the exact grade; and **E3**, discovery/context only.

### 11.4 Ranked shortlist and decision boundary

1. **Vancore B** — strongest direct photonic-assembly function; needs exact-grade TDS and interface-strength/reliability data ([primary experiment](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma17215297)).
2. **IP-L with treated COP** — strongest covalent sealing/interface result; needs current commercial identity and optical qualification ([primary experiment](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-90571-2)).
3. **IP-Dip with treated glass** — quantified anchoring on a relevant optical substrate, but not component attachment ([primary experiment](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0005548)).
4. **PETMP/TTT shape-memory resin** — strongest quantified reversible dry adhesion; lacks photonic and commercial evidence ([primary experiment](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsapm.5c02773)).
5. **UA 9072/BPA(EO)15DMA/TPO** — genuine reversible-adhesive testing; lacks telecom optics and formulated-product status ([primary experiment](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.202103826)).
6. **SU-8 2075** — exact commercial TPP grade, but its bonding evidence remains family-level ([TPP study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optmat.2021.110800)).

**Candidates safe to insert now:** none. **Candidates needing an exact TDS or full-paper/supplement access:** Vancore B (TDS and interface reliability), IP-L (current TDS), SU-8 2075 (exact-grade bonding), and OrmoComp (defined bond test). The PETMP/TTT and UA 9072 mixtures need optical characterization and a reproducible supply/formulation basis rather than a commercial TDS. PETMP/PETA is rejected from this dual-use track because the exact study has no adhesion test.

### 11.5 Smallest justified next database change

Make **no material insertion or schema change** from Phase 3. The smallest defensible later change is an evidence-only update to the existing IP-Dip and OrmoComp records: attach the primary papers while explicitly labeling substrate anchoring/processing as non-adhesive evidence. A new candidate should enter the production corpus only after independent review finds one exact formulation with (a) TPP evidence, (b) a defined bond or attachment test with substrates, geometry, method and strength, and (c) sufficient optical identity to avoid treating device insertion loss as material attenuation.

### 12.3 Completeness denominator audit

The current core metric uses 11 fields for an optical record: Tg, CTE, operating temperature, Young's modulus, bond strength, cure shrinkage, viscosity, cure schedule, reliability, refractive index, and transmission ([current implementation](index.html)). Eight photopolymers have a stated optical role and retain that denominator. UpSol's stated role is structural microfabrication rather than optics, so its two optical fields remain genuinely inapplicable and its denominator is 9. ACOP is assessing possible adhesive use, so bond strength stays applicable and missing for all nine until a real test exists. No field is excluded merely to improve a percentage.

The defect is field interpretation, not denominator size. The adhesive calculation must remain unchanged. For `Structural photopolymer` only, the modulus slot may use the existing `elastic_modulus` field; the process slot may use documented `writing_process` / `development_post_cure_process`; and the optical slot may recognize structured observations without calling graph-only evidence quantitative. Explicit manufacturer-reported dB/cm is comparable reported loss, not a percentage-derived “apparent attenuation.”

### 12.4 Accepted/rejected source policy and next evidence

Accepted sources are exact-grade manufacturer PDFs/pages and exact-product primary experiments with retained test conditions. Product-family brochures, reseller summaries, predictions, and patents without explicit product mapping were rejected. Exact-product studies were also rejected for a field when their result measured a fabricated geometry, decomposition process, or device rather than the requested bulk property ([IP-S mechanics example](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma15124070), [IP-Dip2 decomposition example](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-41535-9)).

**Manufacturer contact is required** for unpublished Tg/method, CTE, liquid viscosity/method, shrinkage, complete processing schedules, specimen-qualified telecom transmission, operating limits, water absorption, thermal cycling, and 85/85 results, with the product-specific gaps listed above. **Original laboratory testing is required** for adhesive candidacy: define substrate pair, surface preparation, bond geometry, bondline/contact area, cure history, conditioning, test method, failure mode, and strength. For optical qualification, measure cured specimens at 1310/1550 nm with numeric path thickness and keep coupling/insertion loss separate from bulk attenuation.
